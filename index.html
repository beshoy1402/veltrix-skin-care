02 · Intelligent assistance

Helpful AI, working quietly in the background.

AI is included where it adds practical value — without making the website feel technical or overwhelming.

For your customers

A personal skincare shopping assistant.

A bilingual chat assistant that helps visitors understand the range, find products and get answers while shopping.

  • Arabic and English support
  • Product discovery and recommendations
  • Ingredient, benefit and usage explanations
  • Routine suggestions based on customer preferences
  • Product comparisons and availability questions
  • Shipping, returns and order FAQs
  • Direct links to relevant products
  • Human support handover when needed
  • Knowledge base using approved brand information

For your team

A business insights assistant.

An assistant inside the admin dashboard that answers questions about store performance and helps your team spot what needs attention.

  • Daily, weekly and monthly sales summaries
  • Revenue, order and product performance analysis
  • Best-selling and slow-moving product insights
  • Low-stock and out-of-stock alerts
  • Customer and order trends
  • Marketing, promotion and bundle suggestions
  • AI-generated business reports
  • Agent activity history and usage monitoring
  • Admin approval for sensitive actions
Example business summary
Top-selling products · Inventory alerts · Sales overview · Suggested next steps

AI guidance will be based on approved brand and product information. It will not provide medical diagnoses or make unsupported medical claims. Actions such as refunds, price changes and deleting records remain under administrator control.
